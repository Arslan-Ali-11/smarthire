Automated Transactional Emails in SmartHire

SmartHire automatically fires transactional notifications whenever:

  • An applicant applies: Instant receipt email to the applicant + new candidate alert with AI score to the recruiter.
  • A recruiter shortlists: Congratulatory email with link to the tailored interview prep guide.
  • A recruiter holds or rejects: Status update email keeping candidates informed.
Steps for Direct Delivery to Your Real Gmail Inbox:
  1. Turn ON 2-Step Verification in your Google Account.
  2. Generate a 16-character App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Paste the credentials into .env file at project root and save!
.env Configuration smtp.gmail.com
MAIL_SERVER=smtp.gmail.com
MAIL_PORT=587
MAIL_USE_TLS=True
MAIL_USERNAME=your-email@gmail.com
MAIL_PASSWORD=abcd efgh ijkl mnop
MAIL_DEFAULT_SENDER="SmartHire <your-email@gmail.com>"
